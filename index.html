Prepare, repurpose and schedule short-form videos from one workspace.

CreatorForge helps creators turn long-form footage into short clips, review the result, add captions, and publish or schedule approved content to connected social media accounts.

WHAT IT DOES

A focused workflow for short-form content operations.

01

Long video to short clips

Import creator-owned footage, identify candidate moments, choose clip duration constraints and render vertical output.

02

Captions and review

Generate readable captions, inspect clip quality and make the final decision before publishing.

03

Connected accounts

Connect authorized creator accounts through official OAuth flows and manage publishing from the local application.

04

Scheduling

Prepare publication metadata, select a time, review the plan and activate approved scheduled posts.

05

Analytics

Show performance summaries for connected accounts and use account-level results to improve future content decisions.

06

User control

CreatorForge does not publish content without the user connecting an account and choosing or approving publication settings.

HOW IT WORKS

Designed around explicit creator approval.

The desktop application runs locally. Third-party platform access is requested only after the user chooses to connect an account.

  1. Import or create contentThe user supplies content they own or have permission to use.
  2. Prepare clipsCreatorForge analyzes the source, proposes short-form edits and optionally adds captions.
  3. ReviewThe user reviews clips, descriptions, visibility settings and scheduling choices.
  4. Publish or scheduleApproved content is sent to the connected account using the relevant official platform integration.
