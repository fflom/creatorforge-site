Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

1. Overview

CreatorForge is a local-first software tool for preparing, editing, scheduling and publishing creator content. This policy explains the categories of information that may be processed when a user connects a supported third-party account or uses related features.

2. Information processed

Depending on the features selected by the user, CreatorForge may process account identifiers and profile information returned by authorized platform APIs, OAuth access and refresh tokens, video metadata, user-selected media files, captions, publishing metadata, publication status and account performance metrics.

3. Local-first processing

The CreatorForge application is designed to run locally on the user's computer. Local project files, generated media and application settings remain in the user's local environment unless the user deliberately sends content or data to an external service required for a selected feature.

4. Third-party services

When the user chooses a feature that requires an external service, relevant data may be transmitted to that service. Examples can include TikTok, YouTube/Google, Meta/Instagram and optional AI or media-processing providers configured by the user. Each third-party service processes information under its own privacy policy and terms.

5. TikTok account data

CreatorForge accesses TikTok account data only after the user authorizes the requested permissions. The application uses authorized data only for the functions shown to the user, such as identifying the connected account, displaying the user's own video information, preparing publication actions and showing account analytics when those capabilities are enabled.

6. OAuth credentials

Access tokens, refresh tokens and integration secrets are treated as sensitive credentials. CreatorForge is designed to keep credentials on the backend/local application side rather than exposing them in browser code. Users can revoke third-party authorization through the relevant platform where supported.

7. Content publishing

CreatorForge does not independently claim ownership of user content. Users are responsible for ensuring they have the rights required to process and publish the media they select. Publication actions occur only after an account has been connected and the user has selected or approved the relevant publishing configuration.

8. Sale and sharing of personal data

CreatorForge does not sell user personal data. Data is shared with third-party services only when required to perform a user-selected function, comply with applicable law, protect the security of the service, or when otherwise authorized by the user.

9. Data retention and deletion

Because the application is local-first, users control local project data stored on their computer. Users may remove local media, application records and connected-account credentials through the application or local storage. Revoking an external platform connection may also be performed through that platform's account settings where available.

10. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to reduce unauthorized disclosure of credentials and locally stored application data. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

11. Children's privacy

CreatorForge is intended as a creator productivity tool and is not directed to children. The service is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when CreatorForge features or integrations change. The date shown at the top of this page identifies the latest revision.
